# Preview

Variation `default`. Rendered by `examples/make_samples.py` from the procedural mannequin (`examples/assets/synthetic_mannequin.mp4`) with palette `neutral-mono`. The subject is drawn. There is no real person in the file.

[Sample](../examples/samples/digital_zoom.mp4)

The frame steps between zoom levels, pixels get blockier, and a generic REC mark, timecode, and zoom readout sit on the picture.

The stills in [`../docs/preview/`](../docs/preview/) are blurred library overviews. They are still images and are not linked to hosted video.
